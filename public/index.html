About Ms. Anna 关于安娜老师

A Firm Believer in Self-Improvement

With over 15 years in education, Ms. Anna (Annie) has taught English language across the globe — from Hong Kong to the United Kingdom. An avid reader, traveller, children's author, and passionate educator, she brings a wealth of knowledge to every lesson.

安娜老师拥有超过15年的教育经验，曾在香港和英国教授英语。她是一位热爱阅读的儿童作家和充满热情的教育者。
